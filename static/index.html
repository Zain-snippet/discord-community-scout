Getting started

  1. Search a permitted public source on Discover, or add a community by hand.
  2. Import a CSV of listings on Communities and review the preview before saving.
  3. Open a community, read its rules, and record whether promotion is allowed, prohibited or unknown.
  4. Generate a tailored draft for a suitable community, review it, and post it yourself if allowed.

Rules that protect you

  • Nothing here joins servers, sends DMs or posts messages. Drafts are text you copy yourself.
  • A community that prohibits promotion is flagged unsuitable with no override.
  • Unknown rules are shown as not verified, never as permission.
